# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with TypeScript and selective React islands, statically deployed to GitHub Pages from the private `YazeKT/yazekt.github.io` repository.

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters, hiring managers, and product or engineering leads evaluating Kirsten Trimaley for remote AI product development, agentic coding, frontend product, or closely related roles. Secondary visitors are technical collaborators reviewing the work and decision-making behind her products.

## Product Purpose

Yaze Media is Kirsten Trimaley's official portfolio. It demonstrates how her graphic-design background, workflow understanding, AI-assisted development, testing, packaging, and publication work combine to produce practical Windows and web products. Success means a visitor understands the positioning quickly, can inspect credible project evidence, and can contact Kirsten about relevant remote work.

## Positioning

Yaze Media builds practical AI-assisted Windows and web products, designed and shipped by Kirsten Trimaley. The site leads with verified work rather than generic AI claims and presents Kirsten honestly as an emerging developer with approximately ten years of graphic-design experience.

## Operating Context

The portfolio is evaluated in browsers on desktop and mobile and is expected to work for fast recruiter scanning, deeper case-study reading, printable resume use, keyboard navigation, and no-JavaScript access. Product proof comes from public repositories, release pages, tests, local verification records, and privacy-safe screenshots.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary action: View the work.
- Secondary action: Contact Kirsten about remote roles.
- Featured projects: dlME, Nestform, Atlas Reach, and PinHarbor.
- Archiva may appear only as a privacy-safe secondary project.
- The site has no CMS, database, analytics, cookies, chatbot, WhatsApp, contact-form storage, public editor, Supabase integration, or Lovable dependency.
- Repositories Kirsten did not build are excluded from all content, metadata, documentation, and credits.
- The old `YazeKT/yazemedia` repository and Lovable deployment remain untouched.

## Brand Commitments

- Yaze Media is the primary brand; Kirsten Trimaley is visibly identified as its founder and AI product developer.
- Voice: direct, grounded, curious, capable, and evidence-led. Never inflate experience, authorship, customers, usage, or results.
- Visual direction: Proof of Work, using real screenshots, release artifacts, annotations, and design decisions rather than generic AI imagery.
- The official launch URL is `https://yazekt.github.io/`.
- Public contact information is limited to the verified public email and GitHub profile.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public YazeKT GitHub profile and portrait.
- Public repositories and release evidence for dlME, Nestform, Atlas Reach, and PinHarbor.
- Verified local validation records and privacy-safe product screenshots.
- No testimonials are approved for migration from the old website.

## Product Principles

- Show proof before making claims.
- Make the work the spectacle.
- Keep authorship and inherited work explicit.
- Preserve product boundaries and privacy.
- Build accessibly, statically, and with minimal client JavaScript.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Essential content and navigation must work without JavaScript. Keyboard focus, readable contrast, semantic structure, responsive layout, reduced-motion behavior, and useful image alternatives are release requirements.
